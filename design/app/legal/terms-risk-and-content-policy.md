# Terms, Risk, and Content Policy

This document summarizes user-facing safety and responsibility boundaries for
the official PaperProof app. It is project documentation, not legal advice.

## Protocol and Labs

PaperProof Protocol refers to the open protocol layer and official deployed
protocol instances. PaperProof Labs refers to the originating team and
maintainer of the official interface, SDKs, reference indexer, documentation,
and brand identity.

The official PaperProof app is one access point to PaperProof Protocol. It is
not the only possible website, client, indexer, or user entry point.

## User Content

Users are responsible for artifacts, files, metadata, comments, votes, links,
citations, attestations, uploads, and other activity they create or submit.
User-generated content does not represent PaperProof Labs unless expressly
identified as official PaperProof Labs content.

Users must not use the app or protocol to publish, upload, link, promote, or
coordinate unlawful, infringing, fraudulent, deceptive, abusive, malicious,
privacy-violating, exploitative, or otherwise harmful material.

## Display Is Not Endorsement

If the app displays an artifact, proposal, comment, vote, address, blob,
external link, or other record, that display does not by itself mean that
PaperProof Labs created, reviewed, approved, verified, endorsed, or recommends
it.

Official app lists, search results, previews, comments, governance views, and
recent activity views are interface features. They should not be treated as
legal, financial, academic, investment, security, or professional advice.

## App Moderation and Non-Display

PaperProof Labs may hide, delist, de-rank, label, refuse to preview, disable
links to, or stop caching content in the official app when content appears to
be unlawful, infringing, fraudulent, malicious, unsafe, abusive, misleading, or
inconsistent with official app policy.

Because PaperProof uses blockchain and decentralized storage infrastructure,
the official app may not be able to delete underlying on-chain records,
third-party records, or Walrus content. Non-display in the official app is an
interface decision, not a global deletion guarantee.

## Wallet and Transaction Risk

Users must review wallet prompts, transaction details, object IDs, addresses,
token amounts, locked funds, governance choices, uploads, and external links
before signing or submitting transactions.

PaperProof Labs cannot recover lost private keys, seed phrases, wallet access,
mistaken transfers, user-approved transactions, or third-party wallet failures.

## AI Copilot Boundary

PaperProof Copilot is a browser-side guidance component. It may help explain
protocol concepts, page context, workflows, and safety checklists. It cannot
sign transactions, custody assets, guarantee correctness, or replace user
review. Users must not paste private keys, seed phrases, wallet recovery
phrases, or confidential credentials into Copilot.

## Reports and Takedown Requests

Abuse, safety, copyright, trademark, privacy, or security concerns may be sent
to:

paperproof.labs@gmail.com

Reports should include enough information to identify the relevant page,
artifact code, object ID, transaction digest, Walrus blob, comment, proposal,
address, screenshot, or external link.
