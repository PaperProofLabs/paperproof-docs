# PaperProof Demo Video Storyboard

Use this reference when planning a 4:40-5:00 PaperProof hackathon demo video.
Target a 4:50 cut and adjust by shortening waits, slide dwell time, or the
closing line.

## Core Message

PaperProof is the missing artifact layer for Sui, Walrus, and agentic software.
It turns durable content into live protocol artifacts with identity, versions,
history, discussion, governance, SDK access, and AI-readable context.

Do not pitch PaperProof as only:

- a storage app;
- a PDF upload page;
- a chatbot;
- a website around a contract.

Pitch it as reusable infrastructure for verifiable knowledge and software
artifacts.

## Narration Tone

Use judge-facing narration:

- concrete, not hype-heavy;
- tied to what is visible on screen;
- focused on what can be verified today;
- clear about the trust boundary between Sui, Walrus, indexers, websites,
  Copilot, and wallets.

Example closing sentence:

```text
PaperProof gives important digital work a verifiable life: durable content on
Walrus, authoritative artifact state on Sui, fast access through SDKs and
indexers, and safe context for AI agents.
```
