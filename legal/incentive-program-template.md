incentives, grants, bounties, contribution campaigns, or participation
recognition programs. It is project documentation, not legal, financial, tax,
or regulatory advice.

Each program should publish its own program-specific terms. This template does
not itself create any entitlement, reward, promise, or obligation.

## Program Purpose

This program is intended to recognize ecosystem contribution and protocol
participation. It is not an investment program, profit-sharing program, wage
program, commission program, dividend program, trading incentive, market-making
program, or guaranteed reward program.

Participation in the program does not guarantee receipt of PPRF, any other
token, cash, grant, future eligibility, official status, endorsement, or other
benefit.

## Eligibility Categories

A program may consider one or more contribution categories, such as:

- useful artifacts published through PaperProof Protocol;
- technical reports, tutorials, docs, translations, or educational content;
- SDK, indexer, frontend, wallet, agent, or analytics contributions;
- bug reports, security reports, reproducible issue reports, or review work;
- governance discussion and protocol improvement proposals;
- community moderation, support, or ecosystem coordination;
- independently operated third-party applications, explorers, dashboards, or
  indexers;
- other contributions identified in the program-specific announcement.

Eligibility should not be based solely on buying, holding, trading, promoting,
or market-making PPRF.

## Non-Guaranteed Eligibility

Any eligibility list, snapshot, indexer output, dashboard, preliminary score,
leaderboard, or community nomination is provisional unless expressly marked as
final by PaperProof Labs or an authorized program operator.

PaperProof Labs may reject, reduce, delay, condition, or remove eligibility for
any participant, address, account, artifact, contribution, or claim at any
time, including after a preliminary list has been published.

## Anti-Abuse Review

Programs should reserve the right to filter or exclude activity involving:

- sybil behavior, duplicate accounts, coordinated farming, or bot activity;
- spam, low-quality, misleading, or machine-generated submissions;
- wash activity, market manipulation, or reward farming;
- plagiarism, copyright infringement, trademark misuse, or impersonation;
- malicious links, malware, phishing, credential theft, or scam content;
- fake engagement, vote manipulation, comment farming, or artificial likes;
- evasion of program rules, jurisdictional limits, sanctions, or prior bans;
- any other behavior inconsistent with program integrity or ecosystem safety.

## Compliance, Jurisdiction, and Taxes

Programs may be unavailable to certain persons, regions, jurisdictions, wallet
addresses, entities, sanctioned parties, or participants subject to legal,
regulatory, tax, reporting, sanctions, or compliance concerns.

PaperProof Labs may request additional information, identity confirmation,
sanctions screening, tax information, attestations, wallet verification, or
other compliance checks before recognizing eligibility or distributing any
reward.

Participants are responsible for their own legal, tax, reporting, and
regulatory obligations.

## Data Sources and Review

Programs should document relevant data sources, such as:

- official deployment manifests;
- Sui transactions, objects, events, checkpoints, or GraphQL/gRPC data;
- Walrus blob references or content availability checks;
- PaperProof SDK validation reports;
- reference indexer outputs;
- manual review records;
- GitHub issues, pull requests, docs, or other off-chain contribution records.

Provider failures, indexer incompleteness, unavailable Walrus content, stale
snapshots, or UI display errors should not be treated as final proof of
eligibility or ineligibility.

## Final Discretion

PaperProof Labs retains final discretion to interpret program rules, resolve
edge cases, correct errors, exclude abuse, modify scoring, re-run snapshots,
change distribution amounts, pause or cancel a program, or take any action it
reasonably considers necessary for legal, security, operational, anti-abuse,
or ecosystem-protection reasons.

Final discretion should be exercised consistently with the program purpose, but
no participant should assume that any action, score, snapshot entry, or
contribution guarantees a reward.

## No Value Guarantee

Any PPRF distributed through a program is subject to the PPRF Token Disclaimer.
PaperProof Labs does not guarantee, maintain, stabilize, redeem, repurchase,
backstop, or protect any price, market value, exchange value, liquidity,
purchasing power, or resale opportunity of PPRF.

## Records to Preserve

For each program, preserve:

- published rules and announcement timestamps;
- eligibility window and snapshot details;
- scripts, query versions, SDK/indexer versions, and manifests used;
- raw data files or reproducible query parameters;
- exclusion criteria and review notes;
- final recipient list and distribution amounts;
- transaction digests for any distribution;
- post-program corrections or appeals handling.

redeem, repurchase, backstop, or protect any price, market value, liquidity,
