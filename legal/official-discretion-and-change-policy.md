# Official Discretion and Change Policy

This document records PaperProof Labs' intended discretion to operate official
interfaces, docs, programs, manifests, policies, and ecosystem processes. It is
project documentation, not legal advice.

## Purpose

PaperProof Protocol is open infrastructure. PaperProof Labs operates official
interfaces, official docs, SDKs, reference indexers, manifests, curated lists,
programs, and communication channels as official project surfaces.

Official project surfaces may need to change because of security issues,
provider failures, legal concerns, abuse, spam, inaccurate data, governance
events, contract upgrades, product changes, ecosystem feedback, or operational
constraints.

## Reserved Discretion

PaperProof Labs may modify, pause, discontinue, rename, reorganize, hide,
delist, de-rank, label, reject, restore, correct, or otherwise change official
project surfaces where it reasonably considers such action appropriate.

This discretion may apply to:

- official websites and app pages;
- official docs and blog content;
- official manifests and curated lists;
- official deployment references;
- official indexer outputs and API surfaces;
- search, ranking, preview, cache, and analytics behavior;
- incentive, grant, bounty, reward, or recognition programs;
- governance-related UI and explanatory content;
- Copilot prompts, guidance, and safety messages;
- brand, logo, and naming guidance;
- public communication and release notes.

## Reasons for Change

Changes may be made for reasons including:

- security, malware, phishing, scam, or fraud risk;
- copyright, trademark, privacy, sanctions, regulatory, or legal concerns;
- sybil attacks, spam, fake engagement, manipulation, or low-quality abuse;
- indexer incompleteness, provider outages, data drift, stale manifests, or
  deployment mismatch;
- Walrus availability, hash mismatch, content corruption, or preview failure;
- user safety, ecosystem safety, or brand confusion;
- contract upgrade, migration, or deprecation;
- error correction, product improvement, or operational simplification;
- community feedback or governance context.

## No Reliance on Continuity

Users and third parties should not rely on any official interface display,
ranking, label, manifest entry, reward estimate, snapshot position, API
response, search result, Copilot answer, docs wording, or program status as
unchangeable.

Official surfaces may change without individualized notice. Where practical,
PaperProof Labs may document material changes, but no user or third party is
guaranteed a particular notice period, transition period, display status,
program result, reward, listing, ranking, or continued support.

## No Endorsement by Action or Inaction

PaperProof Labs' decision to display, hide, label, ignore, investigate, reject,
or take no action on any content, address, artifact, proposal, comment, link,
program entry, or third-party interface does not by itself create endorsement,
approval, partnership, official status, legal conclusion, or duty to act in a
similar way later.

## Documentation and Evidence

For material changes, PaperProof Labs should preserve reasonable records, such
as:

- change timestamp;
- affected content, feature, program, manifest, or policy;
- reason category;
- relevant evidence or issue links;
- operator or reviewer;
- before/after state;
- public notice, if any;
- follow-up correction, restoration, or appeal outcome, if any.

These records are operational records and do not create guaranteed rights for
any participant, user, address, or third party.
