# Protocol, User Content, and Risk Boundaries

This document records PaperProof Labs' intended operating boundaries for
PaperProof Protocol, official interfaces, user-generated content, third-party
interfaces, and abuse handling. It is project documentation, not legal advice.

## Protocol and Labs

PaperProof Protocol refers to the open protocol layer and official deployed
protocol instances. PaperProof Labs refers to the originating team and
maintainer of the official interface, SDKs, reference indexer, documentation,
and brand identity.

PaperProof Protocol is infrastructure. It can be accessed through official
interfaces, third-party interfaces, SDKs, indexers, scripts, wallets, agents,
or direct chain interactions. PaperProof Labs is not the author, publisher,
operator, custodian, adviser, broker, guarantor, or endorser of every action
taken through the protocol.

## User Responsibility

Users are responsible for the content they publish, upload, link, comment on,
vote on, like, dislike, cite, attest to, or otherwise interact with through
PaperProof Protocol or any interface to it.

Users must not use PaperProof Protocol, official interfaces, SDKs, indexers,
or related tools to publish, upload, promote, coordinate, or facilitate:

- unlawful, infringing, fraudulent, deceptive, defamatory, harassing, abusive,
  malicious, exploitative, or privacy-violating material;
- malware, credential theft, phishing, scam, market manipulation, or other
  harmful activity;
- unauthorized personal data, private keys, seed phrases, recovery phrases, or
  confidential material;
- content that violates applicable laws, third-party rights, platform rules, or
  storage/network policies.

PaperProof Labs does not guarantee the legality, accuracy, quality, safety,
availability, academic merit, investment value, or fitness of user-generated
content, third-party content, Walrus content, external links, comments,
governance proposals, AI-generated responses, or third-party interfaces.

## Official Interface Display Is Not Endorsement

Display of an artifact, comment, proposal, vote, link, address, blob reference,
or other record in an official PaperProof interface does not by itself mean
that PaperProof Labs created, reviewed, approved, verified, endorsed, or
recommends it.

PaperProof Labs may operate official interfaces that choose what to show,
hide, label, warn about, rank, cache, index, preview, or refuse to display.
Official interface policy is a product and safety layer; it does not change the
underlying chain or storage facts.

## Open Protocol, Multiple Entry Points

PaperProof Protocol is designed to support multiple frontends, indexers,
wallets, dashboards, agents, academic tools, community portals, and other
ecosystem entry points. Third-party entry points are independently operated.
Their actions, content policies, rankings, moderation decisions, security
practices, wallet integrations, and legal obligations are their own.

Third-party interfaces may factually describe compatibility with PaperProof
Protocol, but they must not imply that they are operated, endorsed, sponsored,
approved, or governed by PaperProof Labs unless separately authorized.

## Abuse, Takedown, and Non-Display

PaperProof Labs may receive abuse reports, copyright complaints, trademark
complaints, privacy complaints, security reports, or other notices concerning
content or behavior associated with official PaperProof interfaces.

Where appropriate, PaperProof Labs may:

- hide, delist, de-rank, label, or refuse to preview content in official
  interfaces;
- disable links, previews, thumbnails, caches, or search exposure;
- document rejected or disputed content in indexer policy layers;
- preserve technical evidence needed for review, security, or abuse handling;
- point reporters to relevant storage, wallet, chain, hosting, or third-party
  interface operators when PaperProof Labs does not control the relevant layer.

Because blockchain and decentralized storage systems can be append-only,
replicated, or operated by third parties, PaperProof Labs may not be able to
delete underlying on-chain records, third-party records, or decentralized
storage content. Interface-level non-display is not the same as global
deletion.

## No Professional Advice

PaperProof Protocol and official interfaces do not provide legal, financial,
investment, tax, medical, academic, peer-review, security, or professional
advice. Users should make independent judgments and consult appropriate
professionals where needed.

## Wallet and Transaction Risk

Users are responsible for reviewing wallet prompts, transaction payloads,
addresses, object IDs, token amounts, locked funds, governance choices,
uploads, and external links before signing or submitting any transaction.

PaperProof Labs cannot recover lost private keys, seed phrases, wallet access,
mistaken transfers, user-approved transactions, or third-party wallet failures.

## AI and Agent Boundaries

PaperProof Copilot and similar agents are guidance tools. They may explain
protocol concepts, page context, safety checklists, and user workflows, but
they do not sign transactions, custody assets, guarantee correctness, or
replace user review. Users must never provide private keys, seed phrases,
wallet recovery phrases, or confidential credentials to any agent.
