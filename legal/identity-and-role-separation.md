# Identity and Role Separation

This document records intended boundaries among individual contributors,
PaperProof Labs, and PaperProof Protocol. It is project documentation, not
legal advice.

## Core Distinction

PaperProof Protocol refers to the open protocol layer and official deployed
protocol instances. PaperProof Labs refers to the originating team and
maintainer of the official interface, SDKs, reference indexer, documentation,
and brand identity.

Individual contributors, maintainers, operators, researchers, developers,
community members, or ecosystem participants may also have personal accounts,
wallets, addresses, social profiles, repositories, experiments, holdings,
opinions, and transactions. Those personal activities do not automatically
represent PaperProof Labs or PaperProof Protocol.

## Official Channels

An action, statement, deployment, publication, release, announcement,
incentive, governance communication, policy change, or operational decision
should be treated as official only when it is made through an official
PaperProof Labs channel or an officially designated authority path.

Official channels may include:

- official PaperProof websites;
- official PaperProof Labs social accounts;
- official GitHub organizations or repositories;
- official documentation repositories;
- official deployment manifests;
- official release notes;
- official email addresses;
- official multisig, treasury, admin, incentive, or operations addresses;
- other channels expressly designated by PaperProof Labs.

PaperProof Labs may add, remove, rotate, or revoke official channels,
operators, representatives, accounts, addresses, or authority paths.

## Personal Accounts and Wallets

Personal social accounts, personal GitHub accounts, personal wallet addresses,
personal experiments, personal transactions, personal comments, personal token
holdings, and personal opinions are not official PaperProof Labs actions unless
they are expressly identified as official through an official channel.

Personal activity must not be treated as:

- an official PaperProof Labs announcement;
- an official PaperProof Protocol governance action;
- an official deployment or upgrade decision;
- an official treasury or incentive action;
- an official endorsement of any content, token, address, interface, project,
  market, or third party;
- an official promise, warranty, investment statement, roadmap commitment, or
  token-value statement.

Third parties should not present screenshots, wallet activity, personal posts,
personal transactions, or informal comments by any contributor as official
PaperProof Labs action unless an official channel confirms that status.

## Representative Capacity

A person may sometimes act in a personal capacity and sometimes act in an
official PaperProof Labs capacity. The relevant capacity should be determined
from the context, channel, authority path, wallet/address used, repository,
signature, announcement wording, and any official confirmation.

Where practical, official communications should use clear labels, such as:

- Official PaperProof Labs announcement.
- PaperProof Labs release note.
- PaperProof Protocol deployment record.
- PaperProof Labs incentive program.
- Personal view; not an official PaperProof Labs announcement.

Failure to include a label does not automatically make a personal statement
official.

## Address and Authority Separation

PaperProof Labs should maintain a distinction among personal addresses,
official admin addresses, official treasury addresses, official incentive
addresses, official operations addresses, and demo/testing addresses.

Official documents, deployment manifests, or operational records should record
which addresses are official for a specific purpose where appropriate. Personal
addresses should not be treated as official merely because they belong to, are
used by, or are associated with a contributor.

## No Implied Endorsement

Following, reposting, liking, commenting on, citing, linking to, transacting
with, or interacting with a third party from a personal account or address does
not automatically imply PaperProof Labs endorsement, sponsorship, partnership,
approval, official support, governance authority, or project affiliation.

## Recordkeeping

For important official actions, PaperProof Labs should preserve reasonable
records, such as:

- official channel used;
- responsible official role or address;
- announcement timestamp;
- relevant transaction digest, release, manifest, or document;
- authority basis;
- any later correction, revocation, or clarification.
