# Takedown and Review Process

This document describes a review process for abuse, safety, copyright,
trademark, privacy, fraud, malware, spam, or other complaints involving
official PaperProof interfaces. It is project documentation, not legal advice.

## Scope

PaperProof Labs may review reports concerning content, links, metadata,
comments, proposals, artifacts, addresses, Walrus references, screenshots,
previews, search results, or other materials displayed or made discoverable
through official PaperProof interfaces.

This process concerns official interface display, indexing, search, previews,
caches, and labels. It does not guarantee deletion of underlying on-chain
records, third-party records, or decentralized storage content.

## Reporting Channel

Reports may be sent to:

paperproof.labs@gmail.com

Reports should include as much of the following as possible:

- reporter name or organization;
- contact email;
- issue category;
- relevant URL;
- artifact code;
- Sui object ID;
- transaction digest;
- Walrus blob ID or URL;
- comment ID, proposal ID, or address;
- screenshots or supporting evidence;
- requested action;
- statement explaining why the content is unlawful, infringing, harmful,
  misleading, abusive, or unsafe.

## Initial Triage

PaperProof Labs may classify reports by urgency and category:

- security or malware;
- phishing, scam, or credential theft;
- child safety or severe abuse;
- copyright or trademark complaint;
- privacy or personal data complaint;
- impersonation or brand confusion;
- spam, manipulation, or low-quality abuse;
- governance abuse or misleading proposals;
- other policy or legal concerns.

Urgent safety or security reports may be handled before non-urgent reports.

## Possible Actions

Depending on the report, evidence, risk, and technical feasibility, PaperProof
Labs may:

- hide, delist, de-rank, label, or refuse to preview content;
- disable thumbnails, previews, links, cached content, or search exposure;
- mark content as disputed, unverified, unsafe, unavailable, or policy-limited;
- remove content from official manifests, curated lists, or recommendation
  surfaces;
- preserve evidence for security, abuse, legal, or operational review;
- contact the uploader, address owner, reporter, or relevant third party where
  appropriate;
- decline action if the report is incomplete, unsupported, abusive, outside
  PaperProof Labs' control, or inconsistent with official policy.

## No Global Deletion Guarantee

PaperProof Protocol uses blockchain and decentralized storage infrastructure.
PaperProof Labs may not be able to delete, edit, reverse, or suppress
underlying on-chain records, third-party data, independently hosted interfaces,
or decentralized storage content.

Official interface non-display is not a representation that the underlying
content no longer exists or cannot be accessed elsewhere.

## Counter-Review and Corrections

Where appropriate, PaperProof Labs may accept counter-reports, corrections,
appeals, additional evidence, or clarifications. PaperProof Labs may restore,
modify, re-label, or continue limiting content after review.

No reporter, uploader, user, or third party is guaranteed a particular review
timeline, outcome, remedy, response, or restoration.

## Repeat Abuse

PaperProof Labs may track repeated abuse patterns, including repeated
infringement, spam, phishing, impersonation, malicious links, fake engagement,
or rule evasion. Repeat abuse may result in additional official-interface
limits, including delisting, de-ranking, label changes, refusal to preview, or
exclusion from official incentive programs.

## Records

For material reports, preserve:

- report timestamp;
- reported content identifiers;
- reporter contact;
- evidence received;
- triage category;
- review notes;
- action taken or declined;
- relevant screenshots or archived records;
- follow-up communications;
- restoration or correction notes, if any.
