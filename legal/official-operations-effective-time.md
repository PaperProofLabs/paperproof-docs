# Official Operations Effective Time

This document records how PaperProof Labs distinguishes pre-launch mainnet
setup activity from formal public official operations. It is project
documentation, not legal advice.

## Mainnet Before Public Launch

PaperProof mainnet contracts, token contracts, objects, packages, events,
transactions, content records, funding movements, authority setup, testing
activity, migration activity, and demonstrations may exist before the official
public launch of PaperProof Protocol.

Mainnet existence does not by itself mean that every pre-launch transaction,
address, funding movement, test, demo, migration, object, or operational
experiment was conducted under the final public official-operations framework.

Pre-launch activity may include:

- contract deployment;
- object initialization;
- package upgrade testing;
- deployment validation;
- SDK and indexer verification;
- website integration testing;
- Walrus upload and retrieval testing;
- governance smoke tests;
- token registration and metadata setup;
- funding, consolidation, migration, or authority setup;
- demo activity;
- operational experiments needed to prepare for public launch.

## Public Launch Effective Time

PaperProof Labs may publish a specific Public Launch Effective Time.

From that time onward, PaperProof Labs intends to apply its formal official
operations controls to official project surfaces, including official
deployment records, official treasury records, official incentive records,
official authority records, official announcements, and official policy-linked
operations.

Until a Public Launch Effective Time is published, PaperProof Labs may treat
mainnet activity as pre-launch setup, validation, migration, testing, or
operational preparation unless expressly stated otherwise through an official
channel.

Suggested record format:

```text
Public Launch Effective Time:
Date:
Time zone:
Announcement URL:
Official website URL:
Official deployment manifest:
Official treasury record:
Official incentive record:
Official authority record:
Notes:
```

## Pre-Launch Interpretation

Pre-launch activity should not automatically be interpreted as:

- final official token allocation;
- official incentive distribution;
- official treasury policy;
- official governance policy;
- final official website policy;
- final official indexer or API policy;
- official endorsement of content or addresses;
- official commitment to repeat a test, demo, migration, transfer, or
  operational pattern after public launch.

Pre-launch records may remain useful for transparency, auditability, and
technical validation, but they should be interpreted in their setup and testing
context.

## Post-Launch Discipline

After the Public Launch Effective Time, PaperProof Labs should apply stronger
operational discipline to official project activity, including:

- maintaining official deployment manifests;
- recording official treasury and incentive movements;
- documenting material authority changes;
- distinguishing personal addresses from official addresses;
- publishing clear rules for incentives, grants, bounties, or airdrops;
- preserving snapshot and distribution records;
- using official channels for official announcements;
- applying content, takedown, risk, token, and discretion policies;
- avoiding unexplained official-to-personal transfers;
- documenting reimbursements, contributor payments, founder allocations,
  migrations, demos, or security rotations where relevant.

## No Retroactive Entitlement

Publishing a Public Launch Effective Time does not create retroactive rights,
claims, rewards, token allocations, official status, endorsement, or reliance
rights for pre-launch activity unless PaperProof Labs expressly says otherwise
through an official channel.

## Updates

PaperProof Labs may update, clarify, or supplement the Public Launch Effective
Time record, official manifests, authority records, treasury records, or
operational policies where needed for accuracy, security, governance,
compliance, or operational reasons.
